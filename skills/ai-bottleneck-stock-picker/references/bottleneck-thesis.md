# Bottleneck Thesis

The strategy starts from AI infrastructure expansion, not from stock themes.

```text
AI demand growth
-> data-center capex
-> physical supply-chain constraints
-> second/third-layer suppliers
-> possible market rerating
```

## Bottleneck Layers

```text
optical:
  Optical modules, optical chips, high-speed interconnect, 800G/1.6T, CPO, silicon photonics.

package:
  Advanced packaging, Chiplet, HBM integration, test, substrates, OSAT capacity.

pcb:
  High-layer PCB, high-speed material, CCL, copper foil, M-sub, signal integrity.

power:
  Data-center power, liquid cooling, transformers, UPS, thermal management.

memory:
  HBM, DRAM/NAND, edge devices, domestic substitution.
```

## What Makes a Good Pick

Prefer names with:

```text
1. Direct exposure to a physical bottleneck.
2. High customer qualification or capacity barrier.
3. Evidence of orders, expansion, pricing power, or financial realization.
4. A-share mapping purity.
5. Manageable valuation/crowding risk.
```

Avoid names where:

```text
1. The thesis is only an AI concept tag.
2. Evidence is only search-keyword noise.
3. Financials do not confirm the story.
4. Recent price action already fully prices the thesis.
5. Risk evidence is stronger than positive evidence.
```
